Who We Are

A Trusted Advisor, Not Just a Contractor.

PMC Build is led by Pat Miazga, who has spent more than twenty years managing construction and civil projects alongside a parallel career in real estate. That combination — knowing how a property is built and how it's valued — is what PMC Build brings to every engagement.

"The right advice before the first shovel goes in the ground saves more than the work itself."

Clients come to PMC Build for judgment and outcomes, not just labour: homeowners planning a renovation, investors evaluating a purchase, acreage owners improving rural land, and developers who need someone credible managing the details on their behalf.
