Full-Scope Home Renovation

Port Coquitlam, BC

Owner-occupied homeClient
Port Coquitlam, BCLocation
CompletedStatus
Interior & exterior renovationTrade

Client Goal

A complete interior and exterior renovation of an owner-occupied home, updating living spaces, finishes, and the exterior envelope.

Challenge

The homeowner remained living on site throughout, which meant sequencing had to minimize disruption to daily life while still keeping the schedule on track.

Approach

PMC Build sequenced interior finishing carpentry and renovation work alongside exterior improvements, coordinating trades room by room and zone by zone rather than opening the whole house at once.

Outcome

A fully renovated home, interior and exterior, delivered on a sequence that kept the homeowner comfortable throughout the build.
