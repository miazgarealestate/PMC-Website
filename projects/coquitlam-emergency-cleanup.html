Emergency Securement & Cleanup

Coquitlam, BC

Coquitlam, BCLocation
3 daysDuration
3 people plus dump trailerCrew
CompletedStatus

Client Goal

Secure a vacant commercial building that was actively being vandalized, and get it cleared out before further damage or loss occurred.

Challenge

No advance warning and a tight window — the building needed securing and a full interior cleanout coordinated on an emergency basis, with sensitive conditions throughout.

Approach

Mobilized a three-person crew with a dump trailer, secured every opening and access point, removed debris and damaged finishes from affected areas, cleaned and sanitized washrooms and the lunchroom, and coordinated follow-up trades including temporary power.

Outcome

Building fully secured and cleaned out in three days, protecting the property from further loss and getting it ready for the next phase of work.

Scope of Work

  • Rapid mobilization on an emergency basis
  • Securing of the building's openings and access points
  • Removal of debris, damaged finishes and refuse from affected areas
  • Cleaning and sanitizing of washrooms and the lunchroom
  • Sorting and bagging of debris to meet disposal facility requirements
  • Coordination of follow-up trades, including temporary power
Project Management: Pat Miazga managed this project as Project Manager through Amkat Services Ltd., coordinating trades, schedule, and quality on-site from start to finish.
