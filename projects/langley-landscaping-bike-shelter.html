Landscaping, Bike Shelter & Repaving

Langley, BC

Canada Post facilityClient
Langley, BCLocation
CompletedStatus
Landscaping, siteworksTrade

Client Goal

Refresh an aging commercial frontage choked with overgrown shrubs and trees, add secure covered bike storage for staff, and repave worn sections of the lot.

Challenge

Three distinct trades — landscaping, structural/fencing, and paving — needed to be sequenced on one active site without the cost and disruption of three separate mobilizations.

Approach

Cleared overgrown bushes and trees and hauled off the debris, graded and rebuilt the planting beds in mulch with a river-stone swale and feature boulders, added new low planting and a replacement tree for open sightlines, built and fenced a steel-frame bike shelter with bike racks, and repaved and re-striped the affected lot areas — all under a single project schedule.

Outcome

A refreshed, low-maintenance frontage with clear sightlines, new secure bike storage, and a repaved lot, completed as one coordinated project rather than three separate contracts.

Scope of Work

  • Removal of overgrown bushes and trees, with debris hauled off site
  • Site clearing and grading of planting beds
  • New mulch beds along the building and parking area
  • River stone swale and feature boulders
  • New low planting and a replacement tree
  • Open sightlines with no concealed areas
Project Management: Pat Miazga managed this project as Project Manager through Amkat Services Ltd., coordinating trades, schedule, and quality on-site from start to finish.
