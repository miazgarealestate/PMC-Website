Rooted in the South Okanagan-Similkameen.

PMC Build is based locally and works throughout the South Okanagan, with project experience across BC when a project calls for it.

Osoyoos, BC

Construction consulting and project management in Osoyoos, including rural and acreage property improvements and coordination with the municipality's ongoing infrastructure renewal work.

Oliver, BC

Renovation planning and project management for homeowners and investment property owners in Oliver, from full interior renovations to targeted property improvements.

Keremeos, BC

Rural property development and construction consulting for acreage owners in Keremeos, including access, site preparation, and outbuilding planning.

Cawston, BC

Rural and agricultural property improvement consulting in Cawston, with attention to the access and site-condition realities of working land.

Rock Creek, BC

Construction project management and rural property development for owners in Rock Creek and the surrounding Boundary region.

Okanagan Falls, BC

Property improvement and renovation planning services for homeowners and investment property owners in Okanagan Falls.

Penticton & Surrounding Area

Construction consulting and owner representation available in the broader Penticton area on a project basis.

Province-Wide, Project-Dependent

PMC Build has managed civil works, retail buildouts, and property improvement projects across BC, Alberta, Manitoba, and Saskatchewan. Larger or specialized projects outside the South Okanagan are considered case by case.
