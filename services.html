Eight Areas of Practice. One Goal: Your Best Outcome.

PMC Build doesn't sell labour by the hour — every engagement is organized around a client outcome, whether that's protecting a budget, evaluating a purchase, or managing a build you can't oversee yourself.

01

Construction Consulting

Independent guidance before you commit to a project — a second, experienced set of eyes on feasibility, scope, and budget before money moves.

  • Feasibility review for a renovation, addition, or new build
  • Contractor bid review and comparison
  • Scope-of-work drafting and clarification
  • Budget sanity-checks before you commit
02

Project Management

Full oversight of trades, schedule, and quality control from permit application through final walkthrough — one point of contact managing every moving part.

  • Trade scheduling and sequencing
  • On-site quality control and inspection
  • Permit and inspection coordination
  • Budget tracking and change-order management
03

Owner Representation

PMC Build sits on your side of the table — managing contractors, reviewing invoices, and making day-to-day decisions on your behalf so you don't have to be on site.

  • Acting as your representative with contractors and trades
  • Reviewing and approving invoices and change orders
  • Attending site meetings and inspections for you
  • Ideal for out-of-town owners and investors
04

Renovation Planning

Scope and sequence a renovation properly before a single wall comes down, so budget and timeline hold up once work actually starts.

  • Defining scope before you approach contractors
  • Sequencing trades to avoid costly rework
  • Realistic budgeting based on current material and labour costs
  • Value-add guidance — what to renovate, and what to skip
05

Property Improvements

Coordinated upgrades — landscaping, structural work, exterior improvements — that add real, defensible value to a property rather than just cosmetic change.

  • Landscaping and site improvement coordination
  • Structural repair and upgrade management
  • Exterior and curb-appeal improvements
  • Multi-trade coordination on a single site
06

Rural Property Development

Acreage and rural land improvements planned around the realities of access, services, septic, and site conditions that don't apply to an in-town lot.

  • Access road and site preparation planning
  • Septic, water, and power coordination
  • Outbuilding and rural structure planning
  • Land-use and site-condition assessment
07

Excavation Coordination

Civil and site work coordinated with the right equipment, crews, and permitting — from a single drainage tie-in to a full parking lot excavation.

  • Drainage, grading, and civil works coordination
  • Equipment and crew scheduling
  • Utility locates and permitting coordination
  • Commercial and municipal site experience
08

Investment Property Consulting

Evaluate a property's real potential and true cost before you buy, renovate, or list it — combining construction cost knowledge with real estate judgment.

  • Pre-purchase condition and cost assessment
  • Renovation ROI evaluation
  • Hold-vs-sell renovation strategy
  • Realistic cost-to-complete estimates
